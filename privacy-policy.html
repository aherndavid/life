What we actually hold about you

There is no database of your work. What we hold is the bare minimum needed to let you sign in and be recognised on your next device:

  • Your email address. This is how sign-in codes reach you, and how the app knows it's you on a new phone or laptop.
  • Your first name, if you've given one. It's only used to greet you, and you can change or clear it under Your account in Settings.
  • Sign-in records. When your account was created and when it was last used. The sign-in service also keeps a short technical log of sign-in attempts, which includes the internet address the request came from. That log is generated automatically as a security measure.

Legal basis: performing the agreement between us. Without an email address there's no way to sign you in, and without sign-in there's no app.

What we do not hold

We cannot see your money, your grades, your deadlines, your timetable, your plans, your notes, your music list, your location or your background picture. Not because we've promised not to look — because none of it is ever sent to us. It lives in your browser's storage on your device.

We have never sold personal data and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else for their own purposes.

Things stored on your device

The app keeps everything you enter in your browser's local storage, under names beginning dpaul_student_. That includes your entries, your settings and theme, your background picture, your cookie choices, and the token that keeps you signed in on that device.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes all of it, as does Delete all my data in Settings. Backup files you save are yours — they're written straight to your device and never pass through us.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which parts of the app get used. It is not loaded at all until you press yes on the banner. Choose no and the script is never fetched, no analytics cookies are set, and any that already existed are deleted.

Nothing you enter in the app is ever sent to analytics. IP addresses are anonymised before storage, and we don't use it for advertising, remarketing or profiling.

Background music and YouTube

The music player is YouTube's, and YouTube sets its own cookies as soon as it loads. So it stays switched off until you press play and agree — before that, nothing is requested from YouTube at all.

When you do agree, the player uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, youtube-nocookie.com, which holds back rather less. It's still YouTube, and what they do with the request is covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one. You can withdraw that agreement under Privacy and cookies in Settings, which stops the player loading again.

Sharing a music list makes a link with the YouTube addresses and titles built into the link itself. Nothing is stored by us, and anyone you send it to can pass it on.

Legal basis: your consent.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub logs the internet address of every visitor to a Pages site for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account. This happens at the hosting layer and is outside our control — we have no access to those logs and cannot see them. GitHub sets its own retention period, described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Sign-in is handled by Supabase, which acts as our processor. Both GitHub and Supabase are based in the United States, so this involves transfers outside the UK, made under standard contractual clauses with the UK addendum.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app available and defending it against abuse.

How long things are kept

  • Your account: until you close it. Ask us and it's deleted.
  • Sign-in security logs: a short rolling period set by the sign-in service, then overwritten.
  • Analytics: retained by Google for 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Your cookie choice: until you clear it, or 12 months, whichever comes first.
  • Hosting logs: held by GitHub for the period in its own privacy statement. We hold no copy.
  • Everything you enter in the app: stays on your device until you delete it. We have no copy to keep.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to how it's used, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time.

In practice we hold very little: an email address, a first name and a last-signed-in date. Everything else is already in your hands, and Back up in Settings gives you a portable copy of the lot in one file.

To close your account or make any other request, email david@dpaul.studio from the address you sign in with. We'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Age

This version of the app is for people aged 18 and over, and we don't knowingly hold data about anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and it will be removed.

Wellbeing links

The app links out to organisations such as the NHS and Samaritans. Following one of those links takes you to their website, where their own privacy policy applies. We are never told that you followed it, and no record of it is kept anywhere in the app.

Changes

If this notice changes materially we'll update the date at the top. Where the change affects cookies, the banner will ask you again rather than assuming your old answer still applies.
