          Google Calendar

          Connect to see your Google Calendar agenda on the Today tab. Read-only: this page can't add, delete or change anything. Nothing leaves your browser except the requests to Google.

          Connecting for the first time? What you'll see
          1. Press Connect Google. A Google window opens. If nothing happens, your browser blocked the pop-up: allow pop-ups for this site and press it again.
          2. Choose the Google account your calendar is on.
          3. If Google says "Google hasn't verified this app", press Continue. This shows while the app is being tested and is expected.
          4. If asked, tick the box to let the app see your calendar events, then press Continue. Without that tick, your agenda stays empty.
          5. Your next 7 days of events appear under Agenda on the Today tab.

          "Access blocked" or "has not completed the Google verification process" means your email needs adding to the app first. Email support with the Gmail address you use and you'll be added.

          Google signs you out after about an hour. When the agenda says your session expired, press Reconnect. To remove access completely, press Disconnect, or go to your Google Account under Security, then Third-party connections.

          Trains and buses

          Live trains leaving in the next hour, in every direction, show on Today. Enter up to three stations as 3-letter codes: MCO is Manchester Oxford Road and MAN is Manchester Piccadilly. Look up any other station's code on nationalrail.co.uk.

          For the Live buses button: open TfGM's live departures page, search for your stop, then copy the address from your browser's address bar and paste it here. The button will then open straight to your stop.

          How trains and buses work
          • Trains: Today lists every train leaving your stations in the next hour, in every direction, in departure order. Each shows its platform, how many minutes until it leaves, and its status: On time, Exp 14:32 (running late, with the new time), Delayed or Cancelled. It refreshes every minute. Disruption notices from National Rail show in yellow at the top.
          • Station codes: every station has a 3-letter code. Manchester Oxford Road is MCO, Piccadilly is MAN, Deansgate is DGT and Victoria is MCV. For any other station, search for it on nationalrail.co.uk and the code shows next to its name. Enter up to three, separated by commas, and press Save and test.
          • Live buses: the yellow button next to Refresh opens TfGM's live departures. To make it open straight to your stop, go to tfgm.com, choose Live departures, search for your stop and open it. Copy the address from your browser's address bar, paste it into Bus times page above and press Save and test.
          • "Live trains aren't available" means live trains haven't been turned on for this copy of the app yet. Live buses still works. Questions? Email support.

            How to use Life - Student Edition

            Your money, deadlines, classes and grades in one place. Everything stays on this device.

            Get started in five minutes

            1. Go to Settings and type your town or postcode, so you get the right weather.
            2. Go to Budget and enter how much is in your bank account and when your next payment arrives.
            3. Add your classes in Timetable.
            4. Add your assignment and exam dates in Deadlines.
            5. If you like, connect your Google Calendar and pick your train stations in Settings.

            Prefer to be shown? Guided set up points to each step with red arrows.

            What each tab does

            Today

            Your home screen. See how much you can spend today, your classes, what's due soon, the next trains, your calendar and a focus timer, all on one screen.

            Budget

            Type in your bank balance whenever you check it. The app takes off bills that are still to pay and tells you how much you can spend each day until your next payment.

            Add your loan and wages under Money in, rent and subscriptions under Bills, and log what you spend.

            Food Shop

            Make a shopping list, add cheap meal ingredients in one tap, and see an estimated total before you go. Tick things off in the shop, then enter your receipt total and it's added to your Budget.

            Tasks

            A simple to-do list. Add a due date and it shows on Today when it's coming up.

            Deadlines

            Add assignments and exams. Each one counts down and turns amber a week before and red three days before. Add to my calendar puts them on your phone with a reminder.

            Timetable

            Add each class once with its day, time and room. Today then shows which class is on now and which is next.

            Projects

            For big pieces of work like a dissertation. Break it into steps with dates, keep notes and save links. The bar fills up as you tick steps off.

            Grades

            Add your modules and marks to see your average and degree classification. Pick the grade you're aiming for and it tells you what you need on the rest.

            Focus timer

            Press Start and work until the chime, then take a short break. After four sessions you get a longer break.

            Music and break reminders

            Press Music at the top and paste a YouTube link to play music while you work. A reminder pops up every 15 minutes to get you moving. You can change or turn it off in Settings.

            Step-by-step help

            Connect your Google Calendar
            1. Go to Settings and press Connect Google.
            2. Choose the Google account your calendar is on.
            3. If Google says "Google hasn't verified this app", press Continue. This is normal while the app is new.
            4. Tick the box that lets the app see your calendar, then press Continue.
            5. Your events now show under Agenda on Today.

            Seeing "Access blocked"? Your email needs adding to the app first. Email support with the Gmail address you use and you'll be added.

            Nothing happened when you pressed the button? Your browser blocked the pop-up window. Allow pop-ups for this site and try again.

            Google signs you out after about an hour. Just press Reconnect when asked.

            Set up trains and buses
            1. Go to Settings and find Trains and buses.
            2. Type your station codes, separated by commas. Manchester Oxford Road is MCO, Piccadilly is MAN, Deansgate is DGT and Victoria is MCV. For any other station, search for it on nationalrail.co.uk and the 3-letter code is shown next to its name.
            3. Press Save and test. Trains leaving in the next hour now show on Today.

            For buses, the yellow Live buses button opens TfGM's live times. To go straight to your stop, open tfgm.com, choose Live departures and find your stop. Copy the web address from the top of your browser, paste it into Bus times page in Settings, and press Save and test.

            Back up your data or move to a new device

            Everything is saved in this browser only. If you clear your browser data, change browser or get a new laptop, it won't come with you unless you've backed it up.

            1. Go to Settings, find Your data and press Back up.
            2. On a computer with Chrome or Edge, choose Choose Where to Auto-save and pick a folder, like OneDrive or Google Drive. Every change is then saved there automatically.
            3. On anything else, press Download Spreadsheet to save a copy. Do this every few days.
            4. To move to a new device, open the app there, press Back up, then Open a Saved Spreadsheet and pick your file.

            Your data and privacy

            Where is my information kept?

            On this device, in this browser only. There's no account, and your budget, tasks, deadlines, timetable, projects and grades are never uploaded to us. If you choose to back up, the file goes wherever you save it, such as your own OneDrive or Google Drive.

            Can dPaul Studio see my data?

            No. We don't run a database, we don't have accounts, and there's no tracking or advertising in the app. We can't see your money, grades, plans or calendar.

            What does Google Calendar access allow?

            Read-only access to your events, so they can show on Today. The app can't add, change or delete anything. Your calendar goes straight from Google to your browser and never passes through us. Access lasts about an hour and is forgotten when you close the tab.

            To remove it completely, press Disconnect in Settings, or go to your Google Account, then Security, then Third-party connections.

            Does anything leave my device?

            Only what's needed to fetch live information, and only when you use that feature:

            • Weather and map: your saved location goes to Open-Meteo for the forecast and to OpenStreetMap for the map. A postcode you search for goes to postcodes.io.
            • Trains: your station codes go through our train service to National Rail. It doesn't record who asked.
            • Music: YouTube videos play from YouTube, which has its own privacy rules.

            Like any website, these services see a normal web request, which includes your internet address.

            How do I delete my data?

            Go to Settings, find Your data and press Delete all my data. Clearing your browser's data for this site also deletes it. Any backup files you saved are yours to keep or delete.

            Is it safe on a shared or university computer?

            Anyone using the same browser on that computer could open the app and see your information. Use your own device or your own browser profile, or press Delete all my data before you leave.
